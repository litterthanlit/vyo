# Exploration A — Pixel-Mosaic Botanical Editorial (`a-mosaic`)

A screen-printed pixel-mosaic botanical editorial poster grammar inspired by Ashmera's *"Small choices create the bigger picture"*, driven directly by real scanned codebase facts (`files`, `facts`, `imports`).

## Visual Grammar

- **Canvas & Grid**: `840 × 1190` logical px rendered at 2× DPR (`1680 × 2380` px) on cool ice-grey ground (`#F1F3F6`), quantized onto a uniform `7 px` mosaic grid (`120 × 170` cells) with crisp paper air between every dot/square.
- **3-Tier Tonal Palette** (100% opaque screenprint inks, zero muddy gradients):
  1. **Pale mist** (`#C0CED9`, `#D5DFE7`) — crisp fine squares (`rx = 0.65px`) for outer petals, background import tendrils, and airy upper-right panicle sprigs.
  2. **Deep slate** (`#345E7A`, `#4B7996`, `#7DA0B7`) — crisp softly-rounded squares (`rx = 0.25 × size`) for tapered stems, splayed botanical leaf sprays, cupped flower calyxes, closed buds, and single berry nodes perched on upper-right sprigs.
  3. **Vivid crimson** (`#CB222B`, `#B21B23`) — jewel-like rounded dots (`rx = 0.41 × size`) reserved exclusively for the focal accent blooms (and a tiny crimson peek in one closed bud), sculpted into asymmetric ruffled petal wings separated by curved pale-mist crevices.
- **Sparse Woven Micro-Type**: Crisp dark charcoal `Commit Mono` (`8.8–9.1 px`) locked strictly to mosaic grid rows across three deduplicated passes:
  1. Single dew words perched on airy upper-right misty twig nodes.
  2. Short plain-language phrases nestled over pale misty blossoms and outer petals (avoiding dark berry collisions).
  3. Bold short core phrases woven sparingly along petal crevices and slate foliage transitions.
- **Editorial Framing**: Wide-open serene top-left negative space for a 2-line `Inter` medium headline (`47 px`, tight tracking), an ultra-restrained 1-line colophon whisper beneath it with real computed counts, and a crisp crimson top-right brand pill (`Inter Display` semi-bold).
